# MyLearning Access Issues

## Category
User Access and Regional Availability

## Product
SAP SuccessFactors Learning

## Incident

A user reports that they are unable to access MyLearning or asks why MyLearning is not available to them.

## Problem Understanding

MyLearning access may depend on the user's applicable population, region, or organizational setup.

Therefore, an inability to access MyLearning should not automatically be treated as a technical defect.

For certain user populations, including users in America or China, MyLearning access may follow different access processes.

---

## Troubleshooting Steps

### 1. Understand the User's Issue

Determine what the user is unable to access and what they are expecting to access.

Confirm whether the issue specifically relates to MyLearning.

### 2. Check User Population or Region

Identify the user's applicable population or region.

Check the applicable MyLearning access process for that population.

### 3. America Users

For users in America who do not have access to MyLearning, inform the user that MyLearning access is not available for their applicable population, if this is confirmed by the organization's access rules.

Do not treat the absence of access as a technical defect when the user is not eligible for MyLearning access.

### 4. China Users

For users in China, MyLearning access may follow a different access process.

Inform the user to access MyLearning through the appropriate company intranet.

If the user has further questions or continues to face issues, advise them to reach out to their appropriate local lead or designated support contact.

Individual lead names or personal contact details should not be hard-coded into the knowledge base because these contacts may change.

### 5. Other Users

For users belonging to populations where MyLearning should be available, continue with the applicable access troubleshooting process.

Investigate the user's access and LMS configuration further if required.

---

## Decision Flow

User reports MyLearning access issue
→ Confirm the issue relates to MyLearning
→ Check user's population / region
→ Identify the applicable access process

If America:
→ Check whether the user is eligible for MyLearning
→ If not eligible, inform the user accordingly

If China:
→ Inform the user to access MyLearning through the company intranet
→ If further assistance is required, direct the user to the appropriate local lead or designated support contact

For other populations:
→ Check whether the user should have MyLearning access
→ Continue with appropriate technical troubleshooting if access should be available

---

## Common Resolution

The resolution depends on the user's applicable population and access process.

For populations where MyLearning is not available, inform the user that access is not provided for their applicable population.

For China users, direct the user to access MyLearning through the appropriate company intranet and contact their local lead or designated support contact if further assistance is required.

If the user should have MyLearning access but cannot access it, continue with technical investigation.

---

## Important Considerations

- Do not assume every MyLearning access issue is a technical defect.
- Check the user's applicable population or region before performing extensive troubleshooting.
- America and China users may follow different MyLearning access processes.
- China users should be directed to the appropriate company intranet for MyLearning access.
- For further China-specific assistance, direct users to their appropriate local lead or designated support contact.
- Do not hard-code individual lead names or personal contact details into the knowledge base.
- If a user should have access but cannot access MyLearning, investigate the issue further rather than assuming access is unavailable.

---

## Escalation

If the user's population should have MyLearning access but the user cannot access it, collect the relevant user and access information and escalate or investigate according to the applicable support process.

For China-specific questions that cannot be resolved through the intranet access process, direct the user to the appropriate local lead or designated support contact.
